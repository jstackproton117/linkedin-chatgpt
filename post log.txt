Why We Spell Ai with a Capital A and a Little i

There’s a reason we write Ai the way we do at Texas Quantitative.

Not AI — the term that’s come to mean artificial intelligence.
But Ai — Applied intelligence.

Here’s the difference:

Artificial Intelligence implies machines are becoming minds.
It borrows the weight of human intelligence — thinking, reasoning, choosing.

But what we work with?
It’s not a mind.
It’s machine intelligence — patterns, probabilities, predictions.

Still powerful. Still transformative.
But not human.

That’s why we call it Applied intelligence:

Capital A for what matters — how it’s used.

Lowercase i for what it is — a machine doing machine things.

Ai isn’t about replacing us.
It’s about amplifying what we care about: insight, action, clarity.

👇 Curious: Do you see AI as something becoming like us, or something best used to support us?
