The common belief is that AI costs are controlled by negotiating better tool pricing. That's incomplete. As of June 2026, more AI services are shifting toward usage-based billing, meaning the real cost driver isn't just the vendor. It’s our engineering workflow.

How many context windows are being filled? How many agent loops run without clear stop conditions? How much data is retrieved before the model ever sees the task? How often are teams paying for repeated reasoning because the system doesn’t preserve useful state?

All these things to think about while budgeting.
