# Drafting Prompt Pack

## Prompt 1 — Turn raw notes into 3 post options
I’m drafting a LinkedIn post. Here are my raw notes and sources:
[PASTE NOTES + SOURCES]

Constraints:
- Audience: engineering leaders, product leaders, builders
- Tone: practical, clear, confident
- No confidential details; anonymize specifics
- 900–1,500 characters (not words)
- Include a strong hook and a clean close (no cheesy CTA)

Generate 3 different post drafts:
1) Story-driven
2) Framework-driven
3) Contrarian take

## Prompt 2 — Improve scannability and hook
Here is the draft:
[PASTE]

Improve:
- Hook strength
- Skimmability (short paragraphs)
- Clarity and specificity
- Remove filler and buzzwords
Keep length within 900–1,500 characters.

## Prompt 3 — Final QA before posting
Here is the final draft:
[PASTE]

QA checklist:
- Any claims that need softening or sourcing?
- Any confidentiality risk?
- Any unclear jargon?
- Any sentence that can be cut?
Return: (1) revised draft, (2) 5-second summary of what changed.
