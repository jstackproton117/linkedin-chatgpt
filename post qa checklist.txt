# Pre-Post QA Checklist

Before scheduling:

☐ No confidential information
☐ No internal metrics
☐ No roadmap references
☐ No vendor contracts mentioned
☐ No security implementation details
☐ Clear hook in first 2 lines
☐ Skimmable formatting
☐ 900–1,500 characters
☐ Signals expertise, not ego
☐ Aligns with one content pillar
☐ No AI-tell phrases or the "leaders should X to Y" pattern (see brand voice guide)
☐ Every number/name/claim in the post actually traces back to the source article
