# LinkedIn Content Ops (ChatGPT + Native Scheduler)

## Goal
Batch-create high-quality LinkedIn posts using ChatGPT for drafting and LinkedIn’s native scheduler for publishing.

## Weekly cadence
- Planning: 30–60 min once per week
- Output: 3–5 posts scheduled for the week

## Workflow
1) Capture idea + sources in a new chat (one post per chat)
2) Generate 2–3 post options (different angles)
3) Select one and polish (hook, scannability, clarity, tone)
4) Final QA (claims, confidentiality, length, CTA)
5) Schedule in LinkedIn (native scheduler)
6) Log what was posted + performance notes

## Rules
- No confidential / proprietary information
- No client names or internal metrics unless public
- Keep posts skimmable: short paragraphs, simple language
- Prefer specific lessons + actionable takeaways
- Be consistent: your “voice” matters more than perfect virality
